# Split the Bill

A small, friendly app for splitting a meal bill with friends in Singapore and Malaysia. A little potato and a mochi keep you company while it does the maths.

**Open the app:** https://timtth.github.io/split-bill/

It runs in your phone's browser. There is nothing to download, no account and no login.

## How to use it

### Split evenly

Use this when everyone pays the same.

1. Pick **Singapore** or **Malaysia**, then tap what the place adds on top (for example **Service charge + GST**).
2. Add your friends under **Who's eating**.
3. Type the amount under **How much?**
4. Everyone's share appears under **Who pays what**.

Typing the final total from the receipt? Pick **Nett prices** so nothing is added twice.

### Split by dish

Use this when people only pay for what they ordered.

1. Pick the country and what the place adds on top.
2. Add your friends.
3. For each dish, type the name, the price and the quantity.
4. Tap who had it. Two names on one dish means they split it. Tap **Everyone** for shared dishes.
5. Each person's share appears at the bottom, with service charge and tax shared out by what they ate.

## Handy extras

* **Who paid first?** Marks the person who paid and shows how much they collect.
* **Copy summary for the group chat.** Copies a ready message with everyone's amount. Paste it into WhatsApp or Telegram.
* **Match my receipt.** In Split by dish, type the final total on the receipt and the shares adjust to add up to it.
* **S$ and RM exchange rate.** Bill in RM but paying back in S$, or the other way round? Switch it on and type the rate.
* **Discount or voucher.** Taken off before the charges are added.
* **Round each share up.** To the next 10 cents or whole dollar for easier transfers.
* **Other.** For any other country. Set your own percentages and currency symbol under **Edit**.
* **New bill.** Clears the bill but keeps your friends.
* **Mascots.** Tap a mascot to swap it.

## What the buttons add

| Country | Button | What gets added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Singapore | Service charge + GST | 10% service charge, then 9% GST on the food and the service charge |
| Singapore | Service charge only | 10% service charge |
| Singapore | Nett prices | Nothing |
| Malaysia | Service charge + SST | 10% service charge, plus 6% SST on the food price only |
| Malaysia | SST only | 6% SST |
| Malaysia | Service charge only | 10% service charge |
| Malaysia | Nett prices | Nothing |

Rates can change over time. You can edit any percentage under **Edit**.

## Put it on your home screen

**iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

**Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Add to Home screen**.

After that it opens from its own icon like a normal app.

## Good to know

* Your friends' names and your bills are saved only on your own phone. Nothing is uploaded or shared.
* Clearing your browser data resets the saved names.
* The app needs internet to open.
